SOLSTICE HOTEL GROUP — FRONT DESK POLICY REFERENCE
For internal use by front desk and guest services associates at all Solstice properties.

This is the quick-reference version of our policies, written for the front desk, not the legal team. If a situation doesn't clearly fit one of these, don't guess, loop in your Manager or AGM.

1. CHECK-IN AND CHECK-OUT TIMES
Standard check-in is 3:00 PM and standard check-out is 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out are both based on same-day room availability. Neither one is guaranteed just because a guest asks nicely, and both are ultimately at the front desk's discretion based on how the house is looking that day.

2. STANDARD CANCELLATION WINDOW
Reservations on our Best Available Rate or a Corporate Negotiated rate can be cancelled free of charge up to 72 hours before the scheduled check-in date. Cancel inside that 72-hour window and the guest forfeits one night's room and tax, charged to the card on file.

3. ADVANCE PURCHASE RATE
Advance Purchase bookings are non-refundable and non-changeable from the moment they're made. This holds even for weather, flight cancellations, illness, you name it. We know it feels harsh in the moment, but the front desk does not have the authority to waive this one. If a guest pushes, the honest answer is that their only real recourse is travel insurance, if they bought it.

4. NO-SHOW POLICY
If a guest hasn't checked in by 11:59 PM on their arrival date, the reservation is marked a no-show and the full first night is charged, regardless of rate plan. This applies even to Advance Purchase bookings that were already paid in full, in which case there's simply nothing further to refund.

5. SERVICE RECOVERY WINDOW
A guest who had a real problem during their stay, noise, cleanliness, something broken, a service failure, has 72 hours after checkout to report it and be considered for a refund, a credit, or a comp night. Past that window, front desk can still offer loyalty points as a goodwill gesture, but not a refund or comp. One wrinkle worth knowing: if a guest mentioned the issue to housekeeping or the front desk *during* the stay, that counts as the complaint being made, and the 72 hours runs from checkout regardless of when they follow up afterward to ask what we're going to do about it.

6. LOYALTY TIER BENEFITS AT CHECK-IN
Silver members don't get an automatic perk beyond earning points. Gold members get a complimentary room upgrade at check-in when one's available, plus late check-out to 1:00 PM subject to availability, same as anyone else. Platinum members get a guaranteed upgrade to the next room class based on same-day inventory, and a guaranteed late check-out until 2:00 PM, no blackout dates and no exceptions. Yes, "guaranteed" here is a stronger word than what we use in Policy 1 for everyone else, that's intentional, it's the whole point of Platinum. What we don't have a written answer for is two Platinum guests both wanting the same last suite or the same 2 PM checkout on a fully booked day. That one's a judgment call for the manager on duty.

7. COMP AND SERVICE RECOVERY AUTHORITY
Front desk associates can comp up to $50 per stay without needing manager approval, things like a waived resort fee, a minibar item, a parking charge. Anything above $50, or a full comped night, needs AGM or GM sign-off. Use good judgment on what counts as "per stay" if a guest has two or three small issues over a multi-night visit that individually are each under $50, add them up before deciding whether you're still inside your own authority.

8. PETS AND SERVICE ANIMALS
Pets are not permitted at any Solstice property, no exceptions, no pet-friendly floors. Service animals, as defined under the ADA, are always welcome and stay free of charge. Front desk can ask what task the animal is trained to perform, but cannot ask for certification, documentation, or a demonstration, and cannot charge a pet fee for a service animal under any circumstance.

9. EXTRA GUESTS AND ROLLAWAY BEDS
Standard rooms are set up for double occupancy, suites for up to four. Rollaway beds are available for a nightly fee and are subject to the room's fire-code maximum occupancy, which the front desk system will flag automatically. Kids under 12 don't count toward the rollaway fee if they're on an existing bed setup.

10. SMOKING POLICY
All Solstice properties are 100% smoke-free, including e-cigarettes and vaping, in guest rooms and indoor common areas both. A violation triggers a cleaning fee, not to exceed $250 and based on housekeeping's assessment of the room, so don't quote a guest an exact number up front, just let them know a fee will apply and the property will determine the amount.

11. LOST AND FOUND
Items are held for 90 days and then donated. If a guest wants something shipped back to them, they cover the shipping cost. We don't reimburse for anything left behind, including high-value items like jewelry or electronics, and there's no exception process for that, it's a straight liability policy.

12. PARKING AND VALET
Parking availability and pricing vary by property, some offer free self-parking, some charge nightly, a couple are valet-only. The front desk system doesn't carry a chain-wide parking rate, so don't state a number from memory, pull the current rate from the property's own fact sheet or point the guest to the front desk team on-site.

13. GROUP BLOCK APPROVAL AUTHORITY
Front desk cannot approve, modify, or discount a group block. That authority sits with Sales and the General Manager, and each property has its own thresholds for room count and discount percentage. Once a block is confirmed, front desk's job is to honor it, check guests in against the rooming list, and flag any discrepancy, not to renegotiate it.

14. ID VERIFICATION AND INCIDENTAL HOLD
Every guest presents a valid photo ID at check-in. For card-paying guests we place a $50-per-night incidental hold; cash-paying guests leave a deposit of two times the nightly rate, refunded within 3 to 5 business days after checkout assuming no damage or additional charges. If the name on the ID doesn't match the name on the reservation, for example an assistant booked it for their boss, don't turn the guest away, just confirm the reservation's confirmation number and note the discrepancy in the reservation file.

15. ESCALATION MATRIX
Anything outside your own authority, comps over $50, refund requests outside the service recovery window, a guest dispute that's getting heated, media inquiries, VIP arrivals, goes to the Manager on duty or the AGM the same day. Anything involving guest safety, a threat, or law enforcement goes straight to the General Manager and Regional Security, any hour, no exceptions, and no need to wait for a manager to be physically on-site first.
